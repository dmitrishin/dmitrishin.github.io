## About Me
I have been working in the construction industry for over 20 years. My experience covers nuclear energy and industrial construction. I have held engineering and leadership positions in design, planning, project management, risk management, and R&D in construction and in artificial intelligence for management and engineering.

I have knowledge in engineering, organization and technology of NPP construction; technical and engineering review of EPC contracts; international laws and regulations (construction, technical regulation, anti-corruption legislation, etc.); IAEA regulatory documents; and risk management. Since 2016 I have been developing competencies in artificial intelligence, machine learning, decision theory, operations research, and systems analysis.

My areas of interest:
- Nuclear power and industrial construction
- Project management and project management methodology
- Risk management, internal control, and compliance in construction
- EPC contract management and engineering contract management
- Artificial intelligence and machine learning in engineering
